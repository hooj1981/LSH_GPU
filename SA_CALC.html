Payment timing

The default

Commission is earned on the date the customer signs and paid up front, unless Commercial Finance says otherwise.

The exception that catches people

If the first delivery date for contracted equipment is expected more than 90 days after signing, then 50% is paid up front and 50% is withheld until the customer begins billing.

!
This means most GPU deals are splitSlide 27 puts minimum GPU delivery at 3–6 months from CD approval. On that timeline the 90-day test is failed by default, so expect half your GPU commission to sit behind first billing.

What "delivered" means now

Historically delivery was satisfied when machines were handed over and billing was set up. Going forward, delivery is complete when the customer pays their first bill to Megaport. That is a meaningfully later trigger than hand-over.

New starter floor

Standard new-starter variable floor is 85% of base salary paid out in commission for the first 3 months. Sourced from slide 21, which sits in the deck's superseded block — confirm with HR before relying on it.

Clawback calculator

If a service is cancelled, decommissioned or terminated before the end of term, Latitude recovers the proportional commission on the unfulfilled portion. This runs the entire contract term with no grace period, and applies even if the early termination fee is waived.

If the ETF is paid, it is your responsibility to raise an exception to get the credit reinstated — it does not happen automatically.
